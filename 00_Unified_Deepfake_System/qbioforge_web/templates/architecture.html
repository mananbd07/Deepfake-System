SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

01. Signal Extraction

Input video is processed to extract Remote Photoplethysmography (rPPG) signals. We isolate the green-channel intensity variations that correspond to human blood flow.

02. Quantum Encoding

Biological features are mapped into a Hilbert Space. We create a quantum state $|\psi\rangle$ representing the physiological coherence of the subject.

03. Hamiltonian Evolution

The state evolves under a Hamiltonian $H$ over time $t$. Deepfakes lack the "biological entropy" found in real humans, causing rapid decoherence in the simulation.

04. Forensic Decision

Final classification based on von Neumann entropy thresholds. Low purity or high noise indicates a synthetic generation artifact.
